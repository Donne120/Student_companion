Student Companion AI ("Student Companion", "we", "us") helps students, alumni, families and universities find answers and opportunities. This policy explains what personal data we collect on each of our platforms, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. It applies to the chat assistant, Pathfinder, the career site, our courses, the university dashboard and our website.

### 1. Who we are

Student Companion AI is the trading name of STUDENT COMPANION AI CHATBOT, a business registered in Rwanda with the Rwanda Development Board (enterprise code 150562967), owned and run by its founder, Andrew Steven Boima. Our office is in Bumbogo, Gasabo, Kigali, Rwanda.

Our founder, Andrew Steven Boima, acts as our Data Protection Officer. For anything about your data, contact privacy@studentcompanionai.rw.

When we provide the service to a university, the university is the data controller: it decides how its students', alumni's and staff's data is used, and we process it on its behalf under a data processing agreement. For example, for African Leadership University users, ALU is the controller and you can also contact ALU's Data Protection Officer. For people who use our services directly (for example Pathfinder or the career site), we decide how the data is used.

### 2. What we collect

**Chat assistant (chat.studentcompanionai.rw)**

- **Account:** your name, email address, profile picture (if you sign in with Google), and the university your email belongs to. You sign in with Google or with an email and password.
- **Your questions:** each question is sent to our servers to be answered. For university reports, we keep a copy with your identity replaced by a scrambled code and with email addresses, phone numbers, links and ID numbers removed from the text.
- **Conversation history:** your past chats are saved on your own device (in your browser), not on our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
- **Files you attach:** photos and documents are read to answer your question and are not stored.
- **Saved opportunities and profile settings:** kept with your account so they appear on any device.
- **"Talk to a person" requests:** if you ask to reach a university team, we keep your name, email, message and the conversation you chose to send, and email them to that team.
- **Daily usage:** how many questions you asked each day, to apply fair-use limits.
- **Email briefing (optional):** if you connect your university Google account, we read recent messages in your inbox to give you a spoken summary. See section 9.
- **Parents and guardians:** your email, the university you joined with its parent code, and when. We never record which student you are related to.

**Pathfinder (for prospective students)**

- Your name, email, phone number, country, current school, the programmes or universities you are interested in, the questions you answer and any message you write, so the universities you choose can contact you. We only send you news from us if you tick the box to agree.

**Career site and courses**

- **Account:** your name and email.
- **Activity:** opportunities you save, and your progress in courses (lessons completed, quiz scores).

**University dashboard (staff only)**

- Staff name, email, role, and a record of changes they make (who did what, when, and from which IP address).

**All platforms**

- Technical data needed to run and protect the service: IP address, browser type, and error logs.

### 3. How we use your data

- To answer your questions and run the platforms you use.
- To route your "Talk to a person" request to the right university team.
- To give universities **anonymised** reports on what students ask (for example the most common topics), so they can improve their services. These reports show counts only: no names, emails or IDs, and a question only appears once at least three different people asked it.
- To show course progress to your university as group totals (for example, how many students completed a course). Groups of fewer than five are not shown exactly.
- To send Pathfinder enquiries to the universities you choose.
- To keep the service secure, prevent misuse, and apply fair-use limits.
- To fix problems and improve answers.

We do **not** sell your data, show you advertising, or use your conversations to train AI models.

**Legal basis.** We process data to provide the service you asked for, on behalf of your university under its agreement with us, with your consent where we ask for it (for example the email briefing or Pathfinder marketing), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

**Your university.** Staff with dashboard access see anonymised reports, and the "Talk to a person" requests you send them. Your university's own privacy policy also applies to what it does with that information.

**Universities you choose in Pathfinder.** Only the ones you select receive your enquiry.

**Service providers** who run parts of the service for us, under contracts that limit their use of your data:

**Others, only when required.** We may disclose data if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business (with notice to you).

### 5. How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Files you attach | Not stored: used only to answer, then discarded |
| Conversation history | On your device until you delete it or clear your browser |
| Question text in university reports | 12 months, then deleted; only anonymous counts by topic and day remain |
| Pathfinder enquiries | 24 months, then your name and contact details are erased |
| "Talk to a person" requests | 12 months after they are resolved |
| Account, saved items, course progress | While your account is active, and 90 days after you ask us to delete it |
| Email briefing access | Until you disconnect it or delete your account |
| Dashboard change records | 24 months |
| Technical logs | 30 days |

When a university's agreement with us ends, we return or delete its users' data as that agreement says.

### 6. How we protect data

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to people who need it to run the service. University staff accounts are created by invitation only and can use two-step sign-in. Reports to universities are anonymised and hide small groups. Email briefing access tokens are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell your university within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

### 7. International transfers

Our servers and most of our providers are in the United States. When your data leaves Rwanda, we do so as Rwanda's data protection law allows, and our providers are bound by contracts to protect it.

### 8. Your rights

Under Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 on the protection of personal data and privacy, Mauritius's Data Protection Act 2017, the EU General Data Protection Regulation where it applies, and similar laws where you live, you can:

- ask for a copy of your personal data;
- correct data that is wrong;
- ask us to delete your data;
- object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time (for example, disconnect the email briefing in Settings);
- not be subject to decisions made only by automated means. The assistant answers questions; it does not make decisions about you, such as admissions or grades;
- complain to Rwanda's National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) or your local data protection authority.

To use these rights, email privacy@studentcompanionai.rw. We will reply within one month, free of charge, after checking that the request comes from you. If you use Student Companion through your university, we may pass your request to the university, since it decides about that data.

### 9. Google user data (email briefing)

The email briefing is optional. If you connect your university Google account, we ask Google for read-only access to your Gmail. We read only recent messages, only to create your briefing; the summary is written by our AI providers and shown only to you. We do not store your emails, do not use them for advertising, do not sell them, and do not use them to train AI models. People at Student Companion do not read them unless you ask us to for support, or the law requires it. You can disconnect at any time in Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Student Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

### 10. Children

If you are under 16, you may use our platforms only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If you are under 18, we recommend a parent or guardian agrees too. Pathfinder is often used by secondary-school students, so before you send an enquiry it asks you to confirm that you are 16 or older, or that a parent or guardian agrees. If we learn we hold data from a child without the required consent, we will delete it.

### 11. Cookies and similar technology

We use only what the platforms need to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your settings, and storing your conversation history on your device. We do not use advertising cookies. The university dashboard uses privacy-friendly visit statistics that do not identify you.

If we make important changes, we will tell you on the platform or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
